Privacy policy

1. Who we are

StoneWars is provided by Yooil Jang (207 Studio). Contact: support page.

2. What the app stores on your device

StoneWars has no account and does not send your game data to a server run by the developer. Your selected map and stone looks, the records of games you finished on this device, and purchase and ad counters are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Nearby play

Nearby play uses Apple’s local networking (Bonjour and Multipeer Connectivity) to connect two devices you choose. Match moves travel directly between those two devices over an encrypted connection and are not sent to the developer. The app asks for local network permission, which you can change in Settings.

4. Advertising

The app can show ads after a match using Google AdMob. According to Google, its Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (used to estimate a general location), a device identifier, advertising data such as the ads you saw, interaction data such as taps, crash logs and performance data such as launch time, to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve the service. StoneWars requests non-personalized ads, does not ask for the App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the advertising identifier for tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. Where the law requires consent, Google’s consent message is shown before ads are requested. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

If the app offers a purchase, it is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

6. Children

StoneWars is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Support requests and this website

If you submit a support request, the information you provide is used to respond to it. The support tracker and this website are hosted by GitHub, whose privacy statement applies. Public issues are visible to others. This site has no analytics or advertising scripts.

8. Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

앱은 대전이 끝난 뒤 Google AdMob 광고를 보여 줄 수 있습니다.
